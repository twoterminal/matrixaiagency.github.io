FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is an AI receptionist? +
An AI receptionist is a voice-based AI assistant that can answer business calls, communicate with customers, answer common questions and capture important information.
Does it work 24/7? +
Yes. An AI receptionist can be available outside normal business hours as well as during busy periods.
Can it answer questions about my business? +
Yes. We configure the AI receptionist using approved information about your business, services, operating hours and frequently asked questions.
Can it capture leads? +
Yes. Depending on your configuration, the AI receptionist can collect information such as the caller's name, phone number, email and enquiry details.
Can it book appointments? +
Appointment workflows can be configured according to your business process and the systems you use.
Are Vapi and phone charges included? +
Third-party AI, telephony, phone number and usage charges are separate from the Matrixaiagency setup and management fees.
